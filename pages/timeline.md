# Timeline

The competition runs as a **single phase**. The leaderboard's public columns
give you live feedback on held-out Mach lines, while the hidden private
columns (other Mach lines, including extrapolation) decide the final ranking.
Up to **5 submissions per day**.

- **Start:** 2026-10-29
- **End:** 2027-02-01 (00:00 UTC)

The final ranking is taken from the private (hidden) relative MAE at the
close of the phase. Because the public and private Mach lines differ, a model
that only chases the public columns will not rank well. Aim for
generalization to unseen flow regimes.
